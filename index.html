05 / The last page

And still,
thank you.

Thank you for the gifts, the laughter, the conversations, the comfort and the memories. Thank you for being a part of my life. I won't pretend that every moment was perfect, but there were beautiful ones, and I am grateful I got to experience them. This is not a request to return or an attempt to change what is. It is simply my way of honouring what was good, and letting it rest. I wish you peace, happiness and a life that is kind to you.

With gratitude, always.
